Water Damage Restoration Service Areas Near Hyattsville, MD

We serve Hyattsville and five neighboring towns along the Route 1 corridor, all part of the Anacostia River watershed.

One corridor, six places

Hyattsville sits on US 1 in Prince George's County. The Gateway Arts and Entertainment District follows that road from the Riverdale Park border south, and the Mount Rainier city site says the district also takes in Brentwood and North Brentwood. Riverdale Park borders Hyattsville at the start of that district, and Brentwood shares the stretch. Mount Rainier is a neighboring city in the same county.

College Park also has US 1 as a main commercial road, and Bladensburg is about 1.4 miles from the center of Hyattsville, measured in a straight line. Calls from all six places go to the same 24/7 emergency line.

The creeks and the watershed

A county nuisance flood plan says Hyattsville is one of eight municipalities along the Anacostia River. Bladensburg's own site says the town sits at the junction of the Northwest and Northeast branches of that river. A county planning study puts Brentwood on the Northwest Branch.

Water runs downhill into the same system, so a hard rain can hit several of these towns at once. Basements near low streets and clogged storm inlets are where it shows up first. Check the FEMA flood map for your address, and ask your city or town about local drainage problems.

What to tell the dispatcher about your address

Every call goes to the same 24/7 line, whichever of the six places you are in. The dispatcher needs a street address and the nearest cross street. It also helps to say whether the water is in a basement, on a main floor, in an upper unit or in a shared hallway. A unit on the third floor of an apartment building is a different job from a flooded basement, and the crew plans for it differently.

If you are not sure which town you are in, say so. Several of these places share ZIP codes and street names along US 1, and a mailing address does not always match the town that issues permits. The dispatcher can sort that out from the street and cross street, so do not spend time hunting for it while water is spreading.

What the crew does first in any of the towns

The first visit follows the same order everywhere. The crew checks for safety hazards, finds and confirms the source, and uses a moisture meter and a thermal imaging camera to see how far the water went. Standing water comes out with pumps and extraction units. Then air movers and dehumidifiers go in, and the crew sets a drying goal based on dry readings from the same materials elsewhere in the building.

The scope of work and the price are explained before work starts, and you can ask questions at that point. What changes from town to town is the building: older rowhouses, mid-century houses and garden apartments each hide water in different places. The town pages cover those local differences, and the services page covers the work itself.

If a leak ran for days before anyone noticed, the answer may include removing wet drywall or insulation. If mold is already visible, mold remediation is handled after the moisture problem is fixed, not before. Your insurance policy may or may not apply, so keep photos and receipts and read your policy or call your agent.

Water that comes from outside the building

Not every call starts with a broken pipe. Heavy rain can push water through a foundation wall or up through a floor drain, and a clogged storm inlet can flood a low street. The city's own stormwater study for the Lower Ward 1 area found standing water on 41st Street during and after intense rain, so rain-driven calls are a real part of the work here.

Water from a storm drain or a sewer is not clean, and it should not be treated like a leaking supply line. Stay out of it, keep children and pets away, and call before you start cleaning. The crew handles contaminated water with different methods, protective gear and disposal steps than a clean-water leak.

ZIP codes we serve

In Hyattsville we serve ZIP codes 20781, 20782, 20783, 20784 and 20785. The nearby towns use their own codes: College Park 20740 and 20742, Bladensburg 20710, Riverdale Park 20737, Brentwood 20722 and Mount Rainier 20712. Each town page explains how its ZIP codes line up with real streets and landmarks, so check yours there.

If your address is not on this list, call anyway. The dispatcher can tell you if the crews can reach you. Same-day response is offered when available, and the line stays open all night.
