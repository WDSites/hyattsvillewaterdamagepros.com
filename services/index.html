Water Damage and Mold Services in Hyattsville, MD

Six services cover a wet home from the first pump-out to the last coat of paint, and one call to the 24/7 line sorts out the order.

How the six services fit together

A water loss moves in stages, and each service covers one stage. Water mitigation stops the damage from growing. Crews extract water, set air movers and LGR dehumidifiers, and track progress with moisture meters.

Water damage cleanup removes wet and contaminated materials such as soaked pad, swollen drywall and insulation. Water damage repair then rebuilds what was removed, from framing and drywall to flooring and paint.

Two services deal with water that is still active or very dirty. Emergency water removal pumps out a burst pipe or a failed appliance. Flood damage restoration covers storm water and sewage backup, which is often Category 3 water.

Where mold fits in

Mold remediation is a secondary service. Mold needs moisture to grow, so it usually shows up after a leak or flood that stayed wet. The EPA says to dry water-damaged areas and items within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth.

If the water source is not fixed and the area is not dry, removing mold only starts a repeat. Remediation follows the S520 standard, with containment, HEPA air scrubbers and negative air for larger areas. For health questions, ask a physician.
